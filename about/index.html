About Dr Sangal

A lifetime spent caring for eyes

Dr Kartikeya Sangal is an ophthalmologist and eye surgeon who has treated patients in Delhi for over 25 years, with a gold medal in ophthalmology and a practice built on clear explanations.

Book an appointment WhatsApp
His approach
“A caring, skilled professional, dedicated to simplifying what is often a very complicated and confusing area of health care.”

That is how Dr Sangal describes his work, and it shapes every consultation. He examines carefully, shows you what he finds, explains your options without jargon and recommends treatment only when it will help.

His practice spans the whole of ophthalmology. Patients come to him for cataract surgery, LASIK and other vision correction, glaucoma, retinal and diabetic eye disease, corneal conditions, squint, eyelid problems and routine eye care for the whole family.

He is a regular contributor of eye-health tips on topics such as lazy eye, red eyes, macular degeneration, astigmatism and headaches linked to the eyes, so that patients can understand their own eye health better.
